  • habebin Auxiliary verb. “gehen” takes “sein” in the Perfekt, not “haben”.
  • meinemeiner Dative case. After “mit”, the possessive takes the dative: “mit meiner Schwester”.
  • zumins Movement into a place. Use the accusative: “ins Kino” (short for “in das Kino”).
